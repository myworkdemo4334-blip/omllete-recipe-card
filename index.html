Simple Omelette Recipe

A quick and easy dish, perfect for any meal. This classic omelette recipe combines tender eggs with fresh spinach and herbs for a satisfying, flavorful bite.

Preparation time

  • Total: Approximately 20 minutes
  • Preparation: 5 minutes
  • Cooking: 15 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2–3 large eggs
  • Salt, to taste
  • Pepper, to taste
  • 1 tablespoon of butter or oil
  • Optional: cheese, chopped vegetables, herbs, or spinach

Instructions

  1. Beat the eggs in a bowl, then add a pinch of salt and pepper and whisk until well combined. You can add a teaspoon of water or milk for a fluffier texture.
  2. Heat the butter or oil in a frying pan over medium heat until melted and lightly bubbling.
  3. Pour the eggs into the pan and tilt it gently so they spread evenly. Let them cook until the edges begin to set.
  4. Add your fillings over one half of the omelette. When the eggs are mostly set but still slightly soft on top, fold the omelette in half.
  5. Cook for another minute, then slide it onto a plate and serve warm.
